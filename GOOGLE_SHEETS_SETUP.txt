GOOZDELISH GOOGLE SHEETS + PHOTO UPLOAD CONNECTION

1. Sign in to Google using Goozdelish@gmail.com.
2. Create a new Google Sheet named: GoozDelish Customer Orders.
3. Open Extensions > Apps Script.
4. Delete any sample code and paste everything from GoogleAppsScript_Code.gs.
5. Click Deploy > New deployment.
6. Choose type: Web app.
7. Execute as: Me.
8. Who has access: Anyone.
9. Click Deploy and authorize both Google Sheets and Google Drive permissions.
10. Copy the Web App URL ending in /exec.
11. Open order.html and replace PASTE_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE with that URL.
12. Save order.html and publish the website.

WHAT HAPPENS
Customer submits order -> text details are stored in the Orders tab -> uploaded reference photo is saved in a Google Drive folder called GoozDelish Order Reference Photos -> the Sheet stores the clickable Drive link in the Photo Reference column -> customer sees the thank-you/deposit message.

PHOTO LIMIT
The website allows JPEG, PNG or WEBP images up to 8 MB.
