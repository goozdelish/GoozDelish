How Your Quote Is Worked Out

Your final price depends on your design/theme, toppers or edible image, flavour/filling, and tiers. Your exact quote depends on:

Servings & size

How many people your cake needs to serve, and the size and number of layers that calls for.

Flavour

Whether you choose a classic flavour or a premium flavour for your cake.

Design & theme

The design and theme you choose, and how detailed or complex it is to create.

Toppers & extras

Any toppers, decorations or extras you would like added to your cake.

Confirming your order

Your order is confirmed only after you receive your quote and a 50% deposit is paid to confirm. Please book at least 10 days in advance whenever possible, as we accept only a limited number of orders.
